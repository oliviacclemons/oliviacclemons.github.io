MEP Building Modeling & Revit As-Built Documentation

Engineering Internship Case Study

Role: Engineering Intern
Tools: Autodesk Revit, AutoCAD, BIM Workflows
Focus: MEP Modeling & As-Built Verification

Project Overview

During my engineering internship, I worked directly with 3D Building Information Modeling (BIM) workflows to translate legacy 2D AutoCAD drawings and engineering documentation into updated, spatially accurate Autodesk Revit models. The primary objective was maintaining high precision across building components to support clear drawing extraction and MEP coordination.

Key Technical Contributions

  • As-Built Model Updating: Translated legacy 2D CAD markups and architectural site plans into dynamic 3D Revit models, ensuring correct spatial orientation and elevational alignment.
  • MEP System Integration: Placed and routed ductwork, mechanical equipment, and electrical fixtures while maintaining proper system connectivity and clearance parameters.
  • Drawing & View Generation: Configured floor plans, sectional cuts, and isometric views to produce clean engineering documentation for field reference.
  • Cross-Disciplinary Coordination: Identified spatial clashes between structural elements and mechanical runs during model refinement, preventing downstream construction conflicts.

Tools & Software Stack

Autodesk Revit AutoCAD BIM Coordination Technical Drawing Interpretation Mechanical & Electrical Systems

Key Takeaways & Engineering Growth

This experience bridged the gap between theoretical drawing interpretation and professional engineering documentation. Operating within real-world BIM standards taught me how vital precision is when coordinating complex building trades, and deepened my proficiency in industry-standard CAD and BIM software.
