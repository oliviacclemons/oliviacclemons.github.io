MEP Building Modeling & Revit As-Built Documentation

Engineering Internship Project Details

Role
Engineering Intern
Tools
Autodesk Revit, AutoCAD
Focus
BIM Modeling & As-Built Documentation

Project Overview

During my engineering internship, I translated outdated AutoCAD drawings and engineering documentation into accurate Autodesk Revit models. The project focused on updating building information models to reflect existing conditions, improving documentation quality, and supporting coordination between mechanical, electrical and plumbing systems.

Development Process

Starting from Existing Drawings

The project began with existing AutoCAD drawings and engineering documentation. These references were used to recreate the building accurately within Autodesk Revit before adding MEP systems.

Building the Architectural Model

Walls, floors, ceilings, and structural elements were modeled to establish the 3D building geometry. Maintaining accurate dimensions was essential before coordinating additional disciplines.

Mechanical System Modeling

Mechanical equipment, ductwork, and associated components were placed and connected within the Revit model to accurately represent the building's HVAC systems. These views illustrate the routing of ductwork, equipment placement, and coordination required to maintain proper clearances throughout the building.

Electrical Coordination

Lighting fixtures, switches, receptacles, and electrical panels were modeled according to project documentation. Reflected ceiling plans were generated to communicate lighting layouts and electrical coordination for construction documentation.

Completed BIM Model

The completed model integrated architectural, mechanical, electrical and plumbing systems into a coordinated BIM environment allowing accurate plans, sections, and isometric views for documentation.

As-Built Documentation

One of my primary responsibilities was developing accurate as-built documentation that reflected existing building conditions. Using the Revit model I created, I produced construction documentation that matched the installed systems and field conditions. The enlarged power plan shown is one example, where every receptacle is labeled with its corresponding branch circuit breaker in the distribution panel, ensuring consistency with the on-site panel schedules and providing accurate documentation for future maintenance and renovations. In addition to electrical documentation, I developed updated plans for architectural, mechanical, plumbing, security, lighting, telecom, and fire/life safety systems.

Project Takeaways

This internship strengthened my understanding of professional BIM workflows, construction documentation, and multidisciplinary engineering coordination. Working on real-world projects improved my ability to interpret technical drawings, develop accurate Revit models, and translate field conditions into reliable construction documents. Through extensive use of Autodesk Revit, I strengthened my skills in 3D building modeling, creating detailed architectural and MEP systems, developing construction documentation, and coordinating designs across multiple disciplines. This experience reinforced the importance of precision and accuracy in BIM workflows, highlighting how well-coordinated digital models and detailed documentation support efficient construction and future building modifications.
