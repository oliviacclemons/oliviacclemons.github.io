Snow Blower Assembly & CAD Design

MEMS 2150 • Mechanical Design Project

Role
Mechanical Designer
Software
SolidWorks
Focus
3D CAD Modeling & Assembly Design

Project Overview

This project involved creating a complete parametric SolidWorks model of a mechanical snow blower assembly. Individual components were modeled from engineering drawings and combined into a fully constrained assembly while maintaining proper fit, clearance, and mechanical relationships between moving parts.

Design Objectives

  • Create accurate parametric models for each manufactured component.
  • Assemble all parts using appropriate mates and mechanical constraints.
  • Verify proper alignment and motion of rotating components.
  • Develop professional engineering drawings from the completed CAD model.

CAD Assembly

The assembly required careful placement of shafts, bearings, fasteners, auger components, and housing geometry. Interference detection and assembly mates were used to ensure components aligned correctly and functioned as intended.

Key Components

Design for Manufacturing

Throughout the modeling process, emphasis was placed on creating parts that reflected real manufacturing methods. Standard hardware, proper tolerancing, and realistic assembly practices were incorporated while maintaining a fully parametric model that could be modified efficiently.

My Contribution

I modeled multiple components in SolidWorks, created engineering drawings, assembled the complete snow blower using mechanical mates, and verified component fit through interference checking. This project strengthened my understanding of parametric CAD modeling, mechanical assemblies, and engineering documentation.
