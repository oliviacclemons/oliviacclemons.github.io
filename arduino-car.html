Team
Six Engineering Students
Tools

Focus
Embedded Systems & Mechatronics

Project Overview

As part of a six-person engineering team, I helped design and build an Arduino-based vehicle capable of responding to spoken voice commands. The project integrated mechanical assembly, embedded programming, and electronic hardware into a single functional prototype capable of navigation, obstacle detection, and real-time user feedback.

System Components

  • Arduino Uno – Controlled the vehicle and coordinated all electronic components.
  • Voice Recognition Module – Allowed the vehicle to respond to programmed voice commands.
  • Ultrasonic Sensor – Detected nearby objects and measured distance.
  • LCD Display – Displayed real-time distance information and system feedback.
  • DC Motors – Powered vehicle movement and directional control.
  • LED Indicator – Displayed the vehicle's parked status.

Project Gallery

Engineering Challenges

Developing the vehicle required extensive troubleshooting to integrate multiple electronic systems into a single platform. Our team resolved power distribution issues, refined wiring connections, and adjusted motor alignment to improve driving performance. Iterative testing ensured the voice recognition, ultrasonic sensor, LCD display, and motor control operated reliably together.

My Contribution

My primary contributions included the mechanical assembly of the vehicle, integration of electronic components, and testing and debugging the connection between the Arduino control code and the vehicle's mechanical systems. I also contributed to the preparation of the final engineering lab report by documenting the design process, testing, and project outcomes.

Project Takeaways

This project strengthened my understanding of embedded systems, electromechanical design, and collaborative engineering. Integrating sensors, motors, and software into a single Arduino-controlled platform provided valuable experience in debugging, system integration, and iterative engineering design.
